In this assignment, you will use Generative AI to build your own application using Vibe Coding (or AI coding). The concept is that you describe what you want to build in natural language, and AI helps you write, test and improve the code. No advanced programming skills are required. Be creative, experiment and see what you can build!

1. What should you build?
Build an application that solves a problem, improves something, or is simply fun to use. You can choose from three approaches:

A. Solve an everyday problem
Think about your life, studies or work. Build a study planner, personal finance tracker, fitness app, booking system, or anything else you find useful.

B. Help a business or organisation
Find something a local business, university or public organisation could improve. Perhaps a restaurant needs an ordering system or a student association needs an event app.

C. Recreate or improve an existing app
Take inspiration from applications you already know:

Social: TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat
Shopping: Finn.no, Amazon, Temu
Transport: Ruter, Uber, Wolt
Banking: DNB, Revolut
Entertainment: Spotify, Netflix, YouTube
Other: NAV, Skatteetaten, Helsenorge, Google Calendar
You don't need to recreate everything. Pick 2–3 important features and make them work. Use existing apps for inspiration, but don't copy proprietary code or impersonate real services.

2. Work individually or in pairs
We encourage everyone to work individually to get hands-on experience. However, you may work in groups of maximum two students, with both contributing actively.

3. Use AI to build your application
You can use any AI tool you prefer, such as, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, GitHub Copilot, Lovable, Bolt.new, Replit, Ollama or SIKT AI chatbot or any other of your choice.

Free versions are sufficient. You are not expected to pay for subscriptions or have a powerful computer. We also shown how to run local AI models using Ollama in our lab. Check Canvas for the material.

Use AI to brainstorm, plan, generate code, test and improve your application.

Your application can be a website, mobile app or desktop application. It only needs to work as a prototype, not be published in an app store.

4. Publishing and submission
We want to see your working application, either through a video or a live demonstration. Submit through Canvas, and include the following:

Demo link (choose only one of the following)
YouTube: Record a video of your application and upload it to youtube. Send us the youtube link (free option) or
VibeOff.aiLinks to an external site.: Ask your AI agent to publish the app on VibeOff. That site will automatically make the video of your application and post it there. Get that link and share it with us (free option) or
Github pages: If static app, post your application on github pages and send us the link (free) or
Use Vercel / Netflify / AWS to host your application and send us the link (could be paid)
Source code (GitHub repository)
Short description of your application and its purpose
Your choice of platform will not affect your evaluation.

Important: Never publish passwords, API keys, confidential information or personal data.

5. Evaluation
The assignment is evaluated as Approved / Not Approved. We will consider your idea, how you used AI, whether the application works, and how you tested and improved it.

We do not expect a perfect application. A simple app with a few working features is better than an ambitious one that doesn't work.

6. Have fun! 🚀
Build something useful, recreate an app you love, or try an idea you've always had.

The goal is not to become an expert programmer. It's to discover how AI can help turn your ideas into working software.

We look forward to seeing what you create!
